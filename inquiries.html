Inquiries

Reach us directly by email. A person reads and answers every message; the same address handles demo requests, general questions, press, and legal inquiries, and we route it internally from there.

Email us

hello@arrivena.com

Use the subject line Arrivena inquiry — [organization] so we can route your message quickly. We reply within one business day.

What to include

A useful first message is short. It helps to include:

  • Your organization's name and the state it operates in
  • Your role at the organization
  • What you currently use to track intake, consent, or referrals, if anything
  • The timeframe you are working against, if there is one

We will follow up with next steps, which typically start with a short call to understand your workflow before any demonstration.

Security and compliance questions

For security questionnaires or compliance review requests, see the Trust Center, or send them to the address above and we will route them to the right person.
